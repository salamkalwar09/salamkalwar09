## 🚀 Currently helping build & grow [Keytomic](https://keytomic.com)

**From 0 → 1 in less than 12 months.**

| 👥 300+ Users | 💰 $20K MRR | 🚀 <12 Months |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Growing customer base | Recurring revenue | From idea to real SaaS business |

I was part of the team that helped take Keytomic from an idea to a revenue-generating AI SEO automation platform.

And my role wasn't limited to writing requirements or telling developers what to build.

I've worked across:

- 💡 **Product ideation & strategy** — Finding the right problems, shaping solutions, and deciding what deserves to be built.
- 🧩 **Feature & workflow design** — Turning messy requirements into flows that actually make sense.
- 🏗️ **System architecture discussions** — Collaborating on how features, integrations, data, and automation fit together.
- 🎨 **UI/UX** — Challenging unnecessary clicks, confusing experiences, and features that need a tutorial to explain themselves.
- 🧪 **Testing & product QA** — Breaking things before customers do. Sometimes quite enthusiastically.
- 📈 **GTM & growth** — Positioning, SEO, AI search visibility, acquisition, experiments, and everything between shipping a feature and getting people to use it.

**The best part?** Working at the intersection of engineering, product, and growth — where an idea becomes something customers genuinely find useful.

## ⚡ A few things I believe

- A feature nobody uses isn't a win just because it shipped.
- Good UX is a growth strategy.
- Distribution deserves as much thought as development.
- AI should make work better, not just generate more of it.
- The best marketing doesn't feel like marketing.
- **Shipping is good. Shipping something people want is better.**
